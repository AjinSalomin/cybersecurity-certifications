# OPSWAT Academy - Critical Infrastructure Protection (CIP)

## About

The course introduced me to the importance of protecting critical infrastructure and understanding the cybersecurity risks that can affect essential services and industrial environments.

## What I Learned

* Understanding critical infrastructure and its importance.
* Why critical infrastructure protection is essential for public safety and national security.
* The potential impact of cyberattacks on essential services.
* The importance of protecting industrial environments and operational technology.
* Understanding the risks associated with attacks on critical infrastructure.

## Key Takeaways

This course helped me understand how cybersecurity extends beyond traditional IT systems. Attacks on critical infrastructure can disrupt essential services, affect public safety, and cause significant financial damage.

As someone focused on cybersecurity, I believe understanding critical infrastructure protection is valuable for building a broader knowledge of security risks and defensive strategies.
